Dana Kim is a researcher and media artist who explores how memories of place are expressed and experienced. Her practice began in documentary filmmaking, and in her master's research she combined it with 3D visualization.

In Quantum Memory Space, she draws on superposition and observation in quantum mechanics as an analogy, training many different times of a single place into one coordinate system with 3D Gaussian Splatting. Her recent work extends this into interactive installations, where the context of the viewer's behavior becomes the observation, and into structures where many people's memories interfere with one another.

Since her undergraduate studies, her interests have centered on cultural phenomena and methods of communication, which she has explored through a series of short films and documentaries, including The Time of Walking in the Forest Path (2023).

She seeks ways to communicate effectively with audiences and to create audience-driven experiences, and plans to expand her methods by focusing on cultural-anthropological phenomena.
